# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative project management approach that emphasizes clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. Our methodology transforms approved initiatives into actionable plans through structured workflows, defined roles, and robust communication channels. From project initiation through retrospectives, every phase focuses on breaking work into shippable increments while identifying dependencies, aligning timelines, and maintaining team capacity. This documentation provides standardized guidance for all team members—developers, project managers, product leaders, and stakeholders—enabling consistent delivery and continuous improvement across every initiative.

## Project Lifecycle & Workflows

OctoAcme's project lifecycle spans six core phases, each with specific objectives and activities. **Project Initiation** turns business needs into approved initiatives by aligning stakeholders and assessing feasibility. **Project Planning** breaks approved work into prioritized backlogs with acceptance criteria, T-shirt sizing, and release timelines. **Execution & Tracking** manages day-to-day work through sprint cycles, quality gates, and risk escalation protocols. **Release & Deployment** standardizes how features ship to production with proper validation and rollback procedures. **Risk Management & Communication** captures risks, assesses impact probability, and ensures escalations reach appropriate decision-makers. **Retrospectives & Continuous Improvement** close the loop by capturing learnings and driving process refinement.

## Roles & Responsibilities

Every project has clear ownership with named roles accountable for specific outcomes. **Project Managers** guide initiatives through all lifecycle phases, manage backlogs, coordinate dependencies, and drive retrospectives. **Product Managers** articulate customer needs, define acceptance criteria, and ensure product value drives prioritization. **Delivery Leads** oversee execution quality, track risks, and own Definition of Done compliance. **Developers** execute committed work, surface blockers early, and participate in planning and retrospectives. Clear role alignment reduces confusion, accelerates decision-making, and creates accountability for both delivery and continuous improvement.

## Communication & Quality

Quality and communication are woven into every process. **Definition of Done** criteria ensure consistent delivery standards before work enters production. **Risk Registers** make dependencies and threats visible, enabling proactive mitigation rather than reactive firefighting. **Weekly syncs** provide a cadence for escalation and cross-team alignment. **Acceptance criteria** in every backlog item clarify success and reduce rework. Retrospectives create psychological safety for the team to surface improvements, building a learning culture that compounds over time.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in every decision
- **Iterative delivery**: Ship small, testable increments to gather feedback and reduce risk
- **Clear ownership**: Every project has named PM and Product Lead with visible accountability
- **Data-informed**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence and customer feedback
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage team members to surface risks, blockers, and improvement ideas
